I am a robotics software engineer working on off-road traversability, motion planning, and learning for robotic manipulation. My research at Worcester Polytechnic Institute’s ELPIS Lab explores how robots can learn from interaction and navigate challenging environments.

I earned my M.S. in Robotics Engineering at WPI in May 2026, and bachelor’s degrees in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at the University of Colorado Boulder in May 2022. Previously, I developed robotic test systems at Naval Nuclear Laboratory.

My recent work spans directional, vehicle-specific traversability mapping, scout-worker route validation, and rapid adaptation for nonprehensile manipulation.
